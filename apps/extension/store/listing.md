## Detailed description

Turn your open tabs into one clean, bookmarked PDF — perfect for weekly readings.

CleanPDF strips the navigation bars, popups, cookie banners and ads out of an
article and gives you just the text and pictures, laid out for reading and
printing.

**Two ways to use it**

- **Save this page (free, offline).** Clean the page you are on and send it
  straight to your browser's print dialog. No account, no sign-in, and no
  network request — the page never leaves your computer.
- **Build a reading pack.** Tick the tabs you want, drag them into the order you
  want, and CleanPDF merges them into a single PDF with a cover page, a table of
  contents with real page numbers, and one bookmark per article. Reading packs
  use the CleanPDF API and need an API key.

**Made for weekly reading**

- Pick the tabs you want and drag to set their order.
- Cover page and contents page, both optional.
- One PDF bookmark per article, so you can jump straight to any piece.
- Three font sizes and three margin widths.
- Include or drop images.
- Name the file after the page or the pack.

**What it does not do**

- It does not read your browsing history.
- It does not touch tabs you have not ticked.
- It does not store your pages anywhere.

## Permission summary shown to users

CleanPDF asks for four permissions and nothing else: `activeTab`, `scripting`,
`tabs` and `storage`. It does not ask for access to every site.
