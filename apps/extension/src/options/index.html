Reading & layout

Applies to locally printed PDFs and to packs built through the API.

CleanPDF account

Only needed for reading packs. Local single-page mode works with no account and makes no network calls.

Where do I get a key?

CleanPDF does not yet expose a public sign-up or key-creation endpoint. Until it does, an operator provisions a key server-side and shares it with you securely; paste it above.
